🔒 Privacy Policy

DSO Empire Simulator

Last updated October 1, 2026

The short version: by default your gameplay save stays in your browser. If you choose to create a free account, we also keep a copy of your save in the cloud (so you can play on other devices) along with your email address. If you appear on the public leaderboard, we store an ID, the name you pick, and your scores. If you buy the optional Legendary Card Pack, your payment is handled entirely by Stripe — we never see your card details. We don't run ads, sell data, or track you across other sites.

Who this is

DSO Empire Simulator is made and operated by Dental Strategy Institute ("DSI," "we," "us"). This policy covers the game at empire.dentalstrategyinstitute.com.

What stays on your device

By default, the game itself — your cash, your hires, your season progress, your settings — is saved only in your browser's local storage and is not sent to us. If you do not create an account and you clear your browser data, switch browsers, or switch devices, that progress is gone; we don't have a copy and can't restore it. Creating an account is optional and changes this, as described in the next section.

Accounts, cloud save and email (optional)

You can create a free account from the "Save your progress" panel on the Leaderboard tab. It works by emailing you a one-time 6-digit code; there is no password. If you do:

  • We store your email address so you can sign in, and a sign-in session in your browser so you stay signed in.
  • We store a copy of your game save in our database, tied to your account, and update it periodically while you play. This is what lets you pick up on another device. Your leaderboard entry is also linked to your account so it follows you.
  • Sign-in emails (your one-time code) are sent only so you can sign in. They are not marketing.
  • Marketing email is separate and off by default. We only send news and offers from Dental Strategy Institute if you tick that box, and we record when you opted in and which version of this wording you saw. You can turn it off any time in the same panel, or use the unsubscribe link in any such email. You can also opt in to event and leaderboard-rank notifications; we only send what you tick.

We use your email address only for the purposes above. We don't sell it or rent it to anyone, and we don't share it with advertisers.

The leaderboard (optional)

If you open the Leaderboard tab, the game may submit your scores (net worth, fastest exit time, IPO shares) to our database so we can show public rankings. What's included:

  • A random ID generated by your browser. If you haven't created an account it isn't tied to your name or email. If you have, it is linked to your account so your entry follows you across devices (your email is never shown on the board).
  • A display name you choose yourself. Please don't put your real name or anyone else's personal information in it — anything you enter here is shown publicly. We run submitted names through an automated filter to catch obvious profanity, but we don't otherwise review what you type.
  • Your submitted game stats and the time they were submitted.

We check submissions for basic plausibility (so the board isn't just whoever edited their save file) — that check looks only at your scores and submission timing, nothing more.

Optional real-money purchases

The game offers one optional real-money purchase: a Legendary Card Pack. If you choose to buy it, you're taken to a checkout page hosted entirely by Stripe, our payment processor — your card number, expiration date, and other payment details go directly to Stripe and never pass through our own code or servers. We only ever receive confirmation that a payment succeeded (and the amount), which our server uses to unlock the cards in your browser. See Stripe's own privacy policy for how they handle payment data.

Who else touches this data

We use a small number of outside services to run the game:

  • Supabase — hosts the leaderboard database, account sign-in, cloud saves and email preferences described above, and sends the sign-in emails.
  • Vercel — hosts the game itself, including the small server-side functions used to process real-money pack purchases. Like any web host, it automatically logs standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. We don't use this for tracking or advertising.
  • Stripe — processes payment for the optional Legendary Card Pack, as described above.
  • Google Fonts — delivers the typefaces used on this page and in the game, which may involve a standard request to Google's servers.

We don't share your information with anyone else, and we don't sell it.

What we don't do

  • No advertising or ad-tracking cookies.
  • No required accounts and no passwords. An account is optional, and sign-in uses an emailed code. We never see, collect, or store your card number or other payment details — Stripe handles those directly, as described above.
  • No selling or renting your information to third parties.
  • No cross-site tracking of you as a person.

Children's privacy

This game is intended for a general business/professional audience and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

If you have an account, you can see and change your email preferences in the game at any time, and you can sign out whenever you like (your save stays on that device). To delete your account, cloud save and email preferences, and to remove your leaderboard entry, use the "Delete my account" option in the same panel or email privacy@dentalstrategyinstitute.com and we'll do it. If you never created an account and want a leaderboard entry removed, contact us with as much detail as you can (the display name you used and roughly when you played) and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in any meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
