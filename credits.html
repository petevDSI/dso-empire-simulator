To Whom It May Concern (probably nobody, this is a video game):

This game began as a slide in an internal deck that got a little out of hand. GoodTimePete wanted a fun way to explain what actually happens inside a dental DSO roll-up — the acquisitions, the "synergies," the EBITDA adjustments that are 60% real and 40% aspirational — without putting anyone to sleep with an actual slide deck.

So instead: a satirical idle game about your dental empire, built by an AI, for an audience of dentists, hygienists, DSO operators, and anyone who has ever sat through a private-equity pitch and thought "this would make a weirdly good game."

If you found this, congratulations — you now know more about this game's origin story than 99% of the people clicking "BILL A PATIENT." Go forth and adjust some EBITDA.

— GoodTimePete, probably from a golf cart
